我们不收集的What we don't collect

App 不读取你的通讯录、位置、相册、麦克风或摄像头，也不扫描你的文件。它只申请三项权限：网络访问（连接服务）、通知（显示下载进度）和前台服务（让下载在后台继续）。App 里没有广告、第三方统计或崩溃上报 SDK。

The App does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and does not scan your files. It asks for three permissions only: network access (to reach our service), notifications (download progress) and foreground service (so downloads continue in the background). There are no ads, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the App.

As with any online service, connection data such as your IP address is processed in transit by the infrastructure providers listed below. Our own download service does not keep access logs.

保存在你设备上的Stored on your device

下载的游戏文件、游戏存档、本地游玩时长和登录状态保存在你的设备上，卸载 App 或清除数据即可删除。

Downloaded game files, save data, local playtime and your sign-in state are stored on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. 我们如何使用信息3. How we use it

  • 提供核心功能：登录、浏览游戏库、下载、记录游玩时长、评分。
  • 计算汇总统计，例如每款游戏的下载量、游玩次数、平均评分和排行榜。这些数字是汇总后的结果，不会显示是谁玩的。
  • 维护服务安全，防止滥用（例如限制异常频繁的下载）。
  • To run the core features: sign-in, browsing the library, downloads, playtime tracking and ratings.
  • To compute aggregate statistics such as per-game downloads, plays, average rating and rankings. These are totals and don't reveal who played.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example, unusually frequent downloads).

我们不会出售你的个人信息，不会用它投放广告，也不会用它做自动化决策来影响你的权利。

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or use it for automated decisions that affect your rights.

4. 公开显示的内容4. What other users can see

你提交的评分和短评会连同你的昵称和头像显示给所有用户。你的手机号永远不会公开。

Ratings and reviews you submit are visible to all users along with your nickname and avatar. Your phone number is never shown.

6. 保存期限6. How long we keep it

账号存续期间我们会保存上述信息。你在 App 内注销账号时，账号以及关联的个人数据（资料、设备、下载和游玩记录、评分与短评）会立即删除；通过邮件申请的，我们会在 30 天内完成。已经汇总、无法再关联到你的统计数字（例如某款游戏的总下载量）会保留。

We keep this information while your account exists. When you close your account in the App, it and the personal data linked to it (profile, devices, download and play history, ratings and reviews) are deleted immediately; requests by email are completed within 30 days. Aggregate figures that can no longer be linked to you (such as a game's total downloads) are kept.

7. 你的权利7. Your choices and rights

  • 修改资料：在 App 的「设置 → 账号」里随时修改昵称和头像。
  • 查看与导出：发邮件给我们，即可获取我们保存的关于你的数据副本。
  • 更正或删除：可以要求删除某条评分、短评或全部数据。
  • 注销账号：在 App 的「设置 → 账号 → 注销账号」中随时操作，账号及关联数据会立即删除。也可以用注册手机号发邮件给我们代为处理。
  • Edit your profile any time in the App under Settings → Account.
  • Access and export: email us for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct or delete: ask us to remove a rating, a review, or all of your data.
  • Close your account any time in the App under Settings → Account → Delete account; the account and its data are deleted immediately. You can also email us, mentioning your registered phone number, and we'll do it for you.

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能还享有其他权利，例如撤回同意或向监管机构投诉，我们会依法配合。

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as withdrawing consent or complaining to a regulator; we'll honour them as the law requires.

8. 安全8. Security

所有通信均经 HTTPS 加密；下载链接经过签名且很快过期；服务器只能访问各自工作所需的最少数据。没有任何系统能保证绝对安全，如果发生影响你的数据安全事件，我们会依法通知你。

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS; download links are signed and expire quickly; each server component can reach only the data it needs. No system is perfectly secure; if an incident affects your data we will notify you as the law requires.

Cartnest is not directed at children under 14, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.
